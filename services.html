Our Services

End-to-end technical execution across the full project lifecycle

What We Deliver

Five Core Engineering Divisions. One Seamless Execution.

Stratech Project Solutions Limited provides full-lifecycle EPICC services — from initial feasibility studies and detailed engineering design through to construction, installation, and turnaround maintenance. Every division operates under strict international standards and our unwavering HSE policy.

  • Maintenance & Turnaround Services (TAM)
01
ENGINEERING

Engineering & Technical Design Services

We provide full-lifecycle engineering design solutions tailored to midstream oil & gas installations, commercial terminals, and industrial structures. Utilizing state-of-the-art software, we convert complex conceptual briefs into buildable blueprints.

  • Feasibility Studies — Comprehensive economic and technical site evaluations, soil mechanics analysis, and environmental assessment
  • Front-End Engineering Design (FEED) — Process flow diagrams (PFD), Piping & Instrumentation Diagrams (P&ID), and equipment sizing specifications
  • Detailed Engineering Design (DED) — Structural calculations, 2D AutoCAD drafting, 3D plant layout modeling, and isometric piping design
  • As-Built Documentation — Field mapping and post-construction drafting verification
Request Engineering Consultation
02
CONSTRUCTION

Tank Farm & Bulk Storage Construction

Stratech is a market leader in the mechanical fabrication and erection of large-scale liquid storage facilities built in full compliance with API 650 standards. From single-shell tanks to complex LPG terminals, we deliver with precision.

  • Storage Tank Erection — Single-shell, double-wall, fixed-roof, and floating-roof storage tanks for PMS, AGO, DPK, Base Oil, and industrial water (up to 28.5m+ diameter)
  • LPG Terminal Construction — High-pressure LPG mounded and spherical bullet installation, pipe rack fabrication, gas hydrants, and truck loading bay gantries
  • Non-Destructive Testing (NDT) — Dye penetrant inspection, radiographic testing (RT), ultrasonic testing (UT), and hydro-testing
  • Aviation Fuel & Bitumen Facilities — Aviation fuel storage & dispensing facilities, bitumen thermal processing units
Request a Project Quote
03
CIVIL & GEOTECHNICAL

Civil & Geotechnical Engineering Works

Industrial storage tanks and heavy machinery demand specialized ground preparation and structural civil support to handle massive dead and live loads. Our civil division handles the most challenging ground conditions across Nigeria's coastal and inland terrains.

  • Ground & Soil Improvement — Hydraulic rotary stone column installation for soft coastal soils (Apapa, Lekki, Warri)
  • Piling & Foundations — Continuous Flight Auger (CFA) piling, driven RC piles, and heavy reinforced concrete ring beam foundations
  • Infrastructure & Civil Works — Concrete dike walls, interceptor pits, weighbridge foundations, industrial drainage, gatehouses, and access roads
  • Housing & Infrastructure — Civil construction for commercial and government infrastructure projects
Discuss Your Civil Project
04
INSTALLATION

Floating Roof Systems & Specialty Installation

We supply and install advanced internal and external floating roof systems designed to minimize volatile product evaporation, enhance safety, and protect stored fuels from contamination. Our installation teams are certified and experienced across all major tank configurations.

  • Internal Aluminum Floating Roofs (IFR) — For light hydrocarbon control and product protection
  • Rim Seal Systems — Primary and secondary rim seal repair and replacements
  • Tank Accessories — Roof leg supports, gauge pole sleeves, and drain system installations
  • Oil & Gas Equipment Procurement — Sourcing and installation of specialized oil and gas machinery and components
Request Installation Quote
05
MAINTENANCE & TAM

Maintenance & Turnaround Services

Minimizing facility downtime while ensuring total operational safety during maintenance turnarounds. Our TAM teams are mobilized rapidly and execute with precision — keeping your assets operational and compliant.

  • Tank Cleaning & Degasification — Comprehensive storage tank cleaning, sludge evacuation, and safe degasification procedures
  • Structural Repair & Re-jacking — Tank bottom plate repair, shell replacement, and structural re-jacking
  • Pipeline Services — Hydro-testing, flushing, corrosion prevention, and protective sandblasting and painting
  • Specialized Machinery Servicing — Backup and turnaround maintenance for specialized oil and gas machinery

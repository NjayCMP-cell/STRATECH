About Us

Our Corporate Story

Built From the Ground Up. Trusted Across the Industry.

Stratech Project Solutions Limited was established in 2017 as Stratech Project Solutions and formally incorporated in 2018 under the Companies and Allied Matters Act 1990 (RC: 1480656). We operate as an indigenous Nigerian EPICC firm specializing in energy infrastructure, industrial mechanical engineering, civil works, and complex project procurement.

Over the years, Stratech has evolved into a strategic partner for multinational energy groups and government entities. Our strength lies in our multidisciplinary team of civil, mechanical, electrical, and petroleum engineers who seamlessly integrate local field execution with international engineering standards.

Health, Safety & Environment

Zero Incidents. Maximum Protection.

At Stratech Project Solutions Limited, Health, Safety, and Environmental management is integral to every phase of our operations. Our policy guarantees the highest standards of personnel protection and environmental responsibility on every project site.

  • Strict adherence to Personal Protective Equipment (PPE) mandates across all project sites
  • Comprehensive risk assessment and Job Safety Analysis (JSA) prior to structural hot work, hydro-testing, or heavy lifting
  • Enforced Substance Abuse and Alcohol-Free Work Environment policies on all operational bases
  • Environmental containment standards during tank maintenance, chemical cleaning, and site preparation

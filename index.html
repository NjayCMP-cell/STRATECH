Who We Are

Providing That Professional Touch to Complex Infrastructure

Stratech Project Solutions Limited is a premier Nigerian Engineering, Procurement, Installation, Construction, and Commissioning (EPICC) service provider. Founded in 2017 and incorporated in 2018, we combine rigorous technical expertise with modern engineering software to deliver complex oil and gas storage terminals, pipelines, civil infrastructure, and specialized turnaround maintenance.

From initial feasibility design to final mechanical commissioning, we ensure seamless execution with zero safety compromises.

What We Do

Core EPICC Service Offerings

End-to-end technical execution across the full project lifecycle

ENGINEERING

Engineering & Technical Design

Feasibility studies, Front-End Engineering Design (FEED), Detailed Engineering Design (DED), 2D/3D AutoCAD drafting, and as-built documentation.

CONSTRUCTION

Tank Farm & LPG Terminal Construction

Heavy mechanical construction of crude, base oil, LPG, and white product storage tanks up to 28.5m+ diameter with floating and fixed roofs.

CIVIL

Civil & Foundation Engineering

Stone column ground improvement, CFA piling, concrete ring beam tank foundations, dike walls, weighbridges, and heavy industrial paving.

MAINTENANCE

Maintenance & Turnaround (TAM)

Tank cleaning, sludge evacuation, NDT testing, pipeline hydro-testing, structural re-jacking, sandblasting and protective painting.
